Thank you for reading and supporting this blog. I spend a great deal of time on it because I want the writing to be useful—to help you skip a few of the detours I have already taken.

Every visit and every share is encouragement to keep going. If the work has helped you and you would like to support it with a donation, I would be sincerely grateful.

Donations go toward books, software, and keeping the site maintained so I can keep publishing carefully written pieces. Any amount is meaningful.

You can give in whichever way is convenient: readers in mainland China can scan a QR code; readers elsewhere can use the buttons below.

## Notes

1. Donations are entirely voluntary; there is no minimum.
2. If you are willing, leave your name and a way to reach you so I can thank you on the donor list.
3. All donations are used to maintain and improve the blog and to buy related books and tools.
4. In mainland China, please use WeChat Pay or Alipay; overseas, PayPal or Ko-fi.

Whether you donate or not: thank you for spending time with the articles, and for walking this path of learning with me.
